# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students, technicians and lab staff who run experiments day to day, usually standing at a bench in bright light, often with gloves on, switching between a protocol, samples and an instrument. Principal Investigators (PIs) and lab managers oversee the same workspace: they approve protocols, decide which parts of the app each student can use, and watch compliance and spending. Most people are not software experts; they want to find the right thing and get back to the bench.

## Product Purpose

LabOS is an all-in-one lab operations platform: protocols and SOPs, samples and freezer storage, instruments and bookings, ordering and inventory, grants, safety and compliance, notebook and meetings. Success is a lab where a new student can find and run an approved protocol in under a minute, a PI can see and control who can do what, and nothing important lives in a spreadsheet or someone's head.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clinical, trustworthy. Quiet and precise, like good lab equipment: no decoration that doesn't help the work. Reference direction: Quartzy (light gray app shell, deep teal actions, small compact type) and Genemod (off-white, restrained, understated blue links, muted until needed).

## Anti-references

- Dense enterprise ERP: walls of tiny controls, dozens of sidebar items shown at once.
- Flashy SaaS template: gradients, glass cards, hero-metric tiles, emoji-heavy chrome.
- Dark "hacker" dashboards: neon on black, hard to read in a bright lab.
- Playful consumer apps: bubbly cards, confetti, cartoonish tone.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the next action, hide the rest.** Default views carry what the person needs now; power features stay one click away (Simple vs Full menu, progressive disclosure), never removed.
2. **Role-aware by default.** A student sees only what their PI allows; a PI sees everything plus the controls to share it. Locked things say why and who to ask.
3. **Calm over clever.** Neutral surfaces, one deep-teal action color, quiet states. Color is reserved for status that matters (approved, overdue, low stock).
4. **Trust through clarity.** Every approval, signature and status is visible with who and when. No ambiguous states, no silent failures; errors say what happened and what to do.
5. **Built for the bench.** Large enough targets, high contrast in bright light, keyboard-friendly, and fast; nothing should make someone wait or hunt.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast (including placeholders and muted text), visible focus states, full keyboard operation, labelled form controls, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support for every animation. Status is never conveyed by color alone (always pair with text or icon).
